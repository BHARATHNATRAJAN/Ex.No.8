# EXPERIMENT 8: Reproducing an Image Using Prompts for Image Generation

### Date: 

### Reg. No : 212223230030
---

## Aim

To demonstrate the ability of text-to-image generation tools to reproduce an existing image by creating precise and detailed prompts. The experiment focuses on identifying important visual elements such as objects, colors, lighting, composition, background, and artistic style and using them to generate similar images.

---

## Tools Used

* **ChatGPT Image Generation (DALL·E)**
* Text-to-Image Prompting

---

# Image 1: Golden Hour Alpine Sunset

<img width="1536" height="1024" alt="ChatGPT Image Sep 6, 2026, 01_56_22 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6664f919-d60d-4c67-a8b6-939eee1dac90" />


## 1. Analysis of the Given Image

The image represents a peaceful alpine landscape during sunset.

* **Objects/Subjects:** Mountains, lake, trees, rocks, flowers and sunset
* **Colors:** Orange, golden yellow, purple, blue and pink
* **Lighting:** Warm golden-hour sunlight
* **Background:** Snow-covered mountains and colorful sky
* **Texture:** Natural rocks, grass, flowers and calm water
* **Composition:** Mountains on the left, lake in the center and sunset on the right
* **Style:** Realistic cinematic landscape photography
* **Mood:** Peaceful, beautiful and majestic

## 2. Basic Prompt

> A beautiful mountain landscape with a lake during sunset.

## 3. Refined Prompt

> A peaceful alpine landscape during golden hour with snow-covered mountains, a calm lake reflecting the sunset, green trees, rocks and colorful wildflowers in the foreground.

## 4. Final Prompt

> A cinematic golden-hour panorama of a tranquil alpine valley with a wildflower-covered meadow and rocks in the foreground, evergreen trees along the slopes, a calm lake reflecting the warm sunset, and tall snow-covered mountain peaks in the background. The sky contains orange, pink, purple and golden clouds with the sun near the horizon. Realistic photography, natural textures, dramatic lighting, deep perspective, high detail and a peaceful majestic atmosphere.

## 5. Generated Image

**Title:** Golden Hour Alpine Sunset

The final image was generated using the refined prompt. It contains the major elements identified from the reference: alpine mountains, lake, trees, flowers, rocks, sunset and warm colors.

## 6. Observation

The generated image successfully reproduced the main characteristics of the reference concept. The composition, sunset lighting, mountain scenery and lake reflection were closely represented. Additional details such as flowers, rocks and trees improved the realism of the generated image.

# Image 2: Cyberpunk Alleyway at Night

## 1. Analysis of the Given Image

The image represents a futuristic cyberpunk city alley at night.

* **Objects/Subjects:** Buildings, neon signs, shops, cables, scooter and a person
* **Colors:** Cyan, blue, purple, pink, red and orange
* **Lighting:** Bright neon lights against a dark environment
* **Background:** Futuristic skyscrapers and holographic advertisements
* **Texture:** Wet pavement, metallic surfaces and old building walls
* **Composition:** Narrow alley leading toward the futuristic city
* **Style:** Cinematic cyberpunk digital art
* **Mood:** Mysterious, futuristic and atmospheric

## 2. Basic Prompt

> A futuristic cyberpunk alley at night with neon lights.

## 3. Refined Prompt

> A rainy cyberpunk alley at night with colorful neon signs, wet reflective streets, futuristic buildings, glowing advertisements and a person walking through the alley.

## 4. Final Prompt

> A cinematic and highly detailed cyberpunk alleyway at night, filled with rain, mist and colorful neon lights. The wet pavement reflects bright magenta, cyan, blue, purple and orange lights. Tall dark buildings surround the narrow street with tangled cables, pipes, vents and glowing signs. A small ramen shop with warm orange lighting is located on the left, while neon signs and futuristic advertisements appear on the right. A lone person walks into the distance toward a futuristic city skyline with a giant holographic advertisement. Realistic textures, dramatic lighting, deep perspective, atmospheric fog and futuristic cyberpunk style.

## 5. Generated Image

**Title:** Cyberpunk Alleyway at Night

The final image was generated using the refined prompt. It includes the major characteristics of the reference concept such as neon lighting, rainy streets, futuristic buildings, signs, reflections and a person walking through the alley.

## 6. Observation

The generated image closely represented the cyberpunk theme. Neon colors, wet pavement reflections, dark buildings and atmospheric lighting created a similar futuristic appearance. Adding specific details such as signs, cables, shops and holographic advertisements improved the overall similarity.

# Prompt Refinement Process

The prompts were developed progressively:

**Step 1 – Basic description:**
A simple description of the main subject was created.

**Step 2 – Add visual details:**
Colors, objects, background and lighting were added.

**Step 3 – Add composition:**
The position and arrangement of important elements were described.

**Step 4 – Add style:**
Realistic cinematic photography was used for the alpine scene, while cinematic cyberpunk style was used for the night alley.

**Step 5 – Final refinement:**
Textures, atmosphere, reflections, lighting and small environmental details were added to improve similarity.

---

# Result

Two images were successfully generated using text-to-image prompting:

1. **Golden Hour Alpine Sunset**
2. **Cyberpunk Alleyway at Night**

The generated images captured the major visual characteristics of their respective reference concepts. Detailed prompts produced better results than simple prompts because they provided information about the subjects, colors, lighting, composition, textures and artistic style.

---

# Conclusion

The experiment demonstrates that text-to-image generation tools can reproduce the major visual characteristics of an image when accurate and detailed prompts are used. Starting with a simple prompt and gradually adding details about objects, colors, lighting, composition and style improved the quality of the generated images. The experiment also shows that prompt refinement and iteration are important for achieving a closer match to a reference image.
